Interpretation and safety

Track lines and pressure contours are experimental deterministic model guidance. Candidate pressure minima may be non-tropical lows, troughs, or numerical features. No calibrated cyclone-track probabilities are calculated from two deterministic models.

Always follow India Meteorological Department (IMD) bulletins for official warnings.
